# Roadmap: from WordPress to mafk-web

The full agenda for the site, what research found, the decisions still open,
and a proposed order of steps. **This is not a plan.** Each step below becomes
its own plan (`/plan`) once its open questions are answered.

## How to use this document

1. Discuss the **open questions** of the next step and record each answer in the
   [decision log](#decision-log) with a date.
2. When a step has no open questions left, start a plan for it. Point the plan at
   the step's section and the decisions it depends on.
3. When a step ships, mark it done in [Steps](#steps) and correct any finding
   that turned out wrong. Findings are dated, so re-check anything older than a
   few months before relying on it.

**Every step with anything visible is also a design task.** Its plan loads the
`frontend-design` skill (with `shadcn`) and follows its process: a short design
plan, reviewed against the brief before building, then a critique in Zen. The
brand tokens (Markaryd green, Inter) are shared with the app and stay fixed.
Step 3 carries the first real design pass: the public site's character.

## What research found (2026-09-19)

### The current site

- **WordPress at Oderland.** LiteSpeed, `A 91.201.60.22`. DNS
  (`dns1–3.oderland.com`) and **mail** (`mx1–4.oderland.com`) are also at
  Oderland. `webmaster@markarydsfagelklubb.nu` is the app's published contact
  address, so the mail must keep working through any DNS move.
- **353 posts** (2010-01-26 → 2026-09-11), about 25 a year, at root URLs
  `/<slug>/`.
  - 229 have inline images, 14 have a featured image, 6 have a gallery and 2 an
    iframe.
  - 131 are Gutenberg HTML and 222 are classic-editor HTML. No shortcodes.
  - Inline images are mostly on the site itself. A few are hotlinked from
    `vinterfaglar.se`, `obj.fotosidan.se`, `tetrix.se` and `protelo.simpleit.nu`,
    and may already be dead.
  - 45 categories, most with 1–2 posts (the big ones: Föreningsaktuellt 105,
    Referat 79, Mötesplats Ekebacken 25, Smålandsrallyt 24). 3 tags.
- **39 pages** at nested URLs `/<parent>/<child>/`. About 20 have real content:
  - Om klubben, Verksamhet, Cinclus, Kontaktpersoner, Medlemsskap, Länkar,
    Fågelholkar, Vinterfågelrally, Smålandsrallyt, Årsprogram 2026, Allmänt.
  - 9 lokal pages (Lokalrapporter) with Artportalen iframes and maps.
  - The app's two legal pages.
  - The rest are empty stubs that redirect to Artportalen.
- **852 media items** (831 listable through the REST API): 732 JPEG, 57 PDF
  (probably Cinclus issues), 30 PNG/GIF and a few Office files. 170 aren't
  attached to any post.
  - **Size: about 0.45 GB.** WordPress doesn't expose file sizes, so they were
    read from HEAD requests: 327 MB across the 601 files that answered, the
    rest extrapolated.
  - Originals are large: the median width is 1280 px and 321 are wider than
    1600 px (the largest 6000 px). The import shrinks them.
- **The host's firewall blocks bursts.** Measuring the media sizes (about 830
  HEAD requests, 6 at a time) got the requesting IP blocked from the server
  while the site stayed up for everyone else (2026-09-19). Anything that
  reads the site in bulk, the import above all, goes one request at a time with
  a pause between requests.
- **Embeds in the content:** Google Maps (9) and Artportalen (5), all on the
  lokal pages, plus natursidan.se and smof.se once each in old posts. No
  YouTube.
- **The "lists" are not generated by WordPress.** The pages are redirects to
  Artportalen, and someone edits the year in them each New Year:

  | WP page | Redirects to (artportalen.se) |
  |---|---|
  | Årslista 2026 | `/List/Top/Species/Fåglar/2026/Kommun/Markaryd/…` |
  | Observatörsligan 2026 | `/List/Top/User/Fåglar/2026/Kommun/Markaryd` |
  | Markarydslistan | `/List/Top/Species/Fåglar/Total/Kommun/Markaryd/…` |
  | Kommunobservationer | `/search/sightings/area/77` |

  The "Obsar" region pages (Skåne, Blekinge, Småland, Halland, Öland) embed
  Artportalen in iframes.
- **Events today are posts** (e.g. "lördagen 19 september – Sträckräkning,
  Skararps ängar") plus a hand-edited "Årsprogram 2026" page.
- **The app depends on two URLs on this domain.** `../mafk-mobie/src/constants/legal.ts`
  links to `/integritetspolicy-markaryds-fagelklubb-appen/` and
  `/anvandarvillkor-markaryds-fagelklubb-appen/`. Both app stores require the
  privacy-policy URL, and "a 404 here is itself a store rejection cause". The
  canonical WP URLs are under `/om-foreningen/…`, so both forms must keep
  resolving.

### The app's data (shared database)

- The app went live **2026-05-29**. Since then:
  - **238 observations** from 18 observers, covering 82 species.
  - 204 of the observations asked for a larm, and **154 have a photo**.
  - 238 `alerts` rows, 21 of them for observations that were later deleted.
- **Most reports are pins, not lokaler.** 211 of 238 observations have free
  coordinates, and only 27 use one of the **61** named `locations`. Alerts
  come from just 13 distinct lokaler.
- **Coordinates are not limited to the kommun.** They range over lat 53.3–56.7
  and lng −6.2–14.3, so at least one is from Ireland. "I kommunen" therefore
  needs a boundary check (see D7).
- `alerts` is a denormalized log: species, lokal and observer **names**,
  coordinates, count and `recipient_count`. It has **no image**. The photo is
  `observations.image_path` in the **private** bucket `user-uploads`: 170
  objects, 86 MB, about 0.5 MB each. The app shows photos through signed URLs.
- `species.rarity` (0 = rarest … 5 = most common) is set on 229 of 231 species.
- **Auth:** 46 users. Identities: email 19 (from the retired web app's magic
  link), Google 18, Apple 14. One admin since step 2.
- **Open issue in the app, not this site:** sign-up is open with
  `mailer_autoconfirm` on. Anyone can create an account with any email address,
  unconfirmed, and any signed-in account can read every member's profile
  (phone numbers included) and all observations. Raised 2026-09-19 and not
  changed, since it's the live app's configuration. Discuss with the club.
- **No PostGIS**, no `pg_trgm`, no `pg_cron`, no `pg_net`.
- **Members-only today.** Every existing table is readable by `authenticated`
  only, and the photo bucket is private. Nothing is readable anonymously, so
  anything the public site shows needs a server-side read (see D1).
- **Privacy policy (the app's, published on the WP site).** It says: *"Andra
  medlemmar i appen ser dina observationer inklusive art, plats, ev. foto och
  kommentar, samt ditt visningsnamn och – om du angett det – telefonnummer."*
  Members agreed to share with **members**. Publishing names, photos or exact
  places on the open web goes beyond that.
- **The larm pipeline could be reused from the web as it is.** The app inserts
  the observation (RLS: own rows) and then invokes `send-bird-alert`. It isn't
  wanted: the site never sends alerts (decided 2026-09-19).

### Artportalen

svalan.se is the old name. Everything is Artportalen now, run by SLU
Artdatabanken. Read from the SOS docs and source code (open source,
[biodiversitydata-se/SOS](https://github.com/biodiversitydata-se/SOS), release
2026.09.04). The API itself is untested, because it needs a key.

- **The official API is the Species Observation System (SOS).**
  - Base URL: `https://api.artdatabanken.se/species-observation-system/v1/`.
    Confirmed live: without a key it answers 401, "missing subscription key".
  - Access: a free account at
    [api-portal.artdatabanken.se](https://api-portal.artdatabanken.se/),
    subscribed to the product *"Species Observations – multiple data
    resources"*. Every request needs the key in the
    `Ocp-Apim-Subscription-Key` header. **Getting the key is a human step.**
  - The full OpenAPI spec is only downloadable from the portal after signing in.
  - SLU recommends calling it from a backend, never from the browser. That fits
    a Worker route with the key as a Worker secret.
- **`POST /Observations/TaxonAggregation` returns exactly what both lists
  need.** One row per taxon: `taxonId`, `observationCount`, `firstSighting` and
  `lastSighting`, for any search filter.
  - Årslista = the kommun's birds between 1 January and today, sorted by
    `firstSighting`.
  - Markarydslistan = the same with no date filter.
  - `take` omitted returns every taxon in one call (the paged form stops at
    1 000).
  - Filter: `geographics.areas: [{areaType: "Municipality", featureId: "767"}]`
    (kommunkod without the leading zero; the docs use Tranås `687` and Kronoberg
    `7`). Birds: `taxon: {ids: [4000104], includeUnderlyingTaxa: true}`
    (class Aves). Artportalen only: `dataProvider: {ids: [1]}`.
- **It returns ids, not names.** Names need a lookup:
  - a static bird-name map (taxon id → Swedish and scientific name) built once
    from Dyntaxa and kept in the repo;
  - an occasional lookup for an id the map doesn't know.
- **Fresh:** Artportalen is harvested incrementally every 5 minutes, so a report
  shows up in SOS within about 5 minutes.
- **Licence:** Artportalen's dataset is **CC0** (per GBIF). Credit "Källa:
  Artportalen, SLU Artdatabanken" anyway.
- **The kommun boundary is in the API too.**
  `GET /Areas/Municipality/767/Export` returns the kommun polygon, which is
  what D7 needs for Larmligan.
- **The catch: protected observations are hidden.**
  - The public index leaves out protected (skyddsklassade) observations.
  - Observations the reporter chose to blur have their coordinates diffused by
    100–1 000 m.
  - Protected data needs an SLU login (OAuth, scope
    `SOS.Observations.Protected`) and granted permission, which a club website
    won't get.
  - **Artportalen's own lists do include protected species.** The current
    Markarydslistan has Berguv, Pilgrimsfalk, Jaktfalk and Lappuggla. So an
    SOS-built list may lack a few species or show later first dates.
- **The baseline to test against** (Artportalen, fetched 2026-09-19):
  - Årslista 2026: **148 species**, latest addition Ljungpipare, 9 aug.
  - Markarydslistan: **229 species**, latest addition Rostgumpsvala,
    26 apr 2026.
- **Observatörsligan can't come from an aggregate.** Nothing aggregates by
  observer. It would mean pulling every bird observation in the kommun for the
  year (`Observations_SearchByCursor`, up to 2 million) and grouping by
  `occurrence.recordedBy`. It isn't on the agenda.
- Scraping artportalen.se is the fallback, and a poor one: it's fragile, and the
  user list loads by script.

## Cross-cutting decisions

These come before the features that depend on them. Each has a recommendation;
nothing is decided until it's in the log.

### D1 — What the public sees of the app's data

**Decided 2026-09-19:** only one administrator signs in on the web, and members
don't sign in at all (see D2). Everything the site shows from the app's data is
therefore **public**: the larm feed, the lokal map and Larmligan.

That conflicts with the app's privacy policy, which promises that only **other
members** see a member's observations, photos, name and place. Before any of it
goes public:

- **The club updates the privacy policy and tells the members** which larm
  details the website publishes. Reports made under the old promise need a
  decision too:
  - publish only reports made after the change, or
  - publish everything, but give members a way to opt out first.
- **Show the minimum.** Recommended public fields:

  | Field | Public? |
  |---|---|
  | Species, count, date, rarity | Yes |
  | Place | Lokal name. A pin is shown as the nearest lokal, or rounded to ~1 km, **never exact** |
  | Photo | Yes, if the policy says so. App photos are re-encoded with EXIF stripped, so they carry no hidden GPS; check any older upload first |
  | Observer's display name | Only if the policy says so; otherwise "en medlem" |
  | Comment (free text) | No |
  | Phone, user id, avatar | Never |

- **Protected species** (nesting raptors, owls and similar) never get a public
  place more precise than the kommun, or they are left out. The app has no
  sensitivity flag, and `rarity` measures something else, so this needs a list
  of its own, e.g. following Artportalen's protected species.
- **How it's served** (no change to the shared database):
  - Nothing there is readable anonymously, and the photo bucket is private. The
    Worker reads with a **secret key**, the site's first Worker secret.
  - The reading happens in one small module that maps rows to public DTOs. A
    test pins the DTO fields, so a phone number can't leak by accident.
  - Photos go through a Worker route (`/larm/bild/<id>`) instead of signed
    URLs. A signed URL would expose the storage path, which contains the
    member's user id, and would expire while a cached page still points at it.
  - Responses are cached for about a minute.
- **Answered 2026-09-20, when step 4 was built:**
  - **The policy update is not a blocker for shipping**, because the site is an
    unlisted `workers.dev` URL carrying `noindex` and a `robots.txt` that
    disallows everything. It moves to the [launch checklist](#launch-checklist-step-9)
    and must go out **before the domain cutover**.
  - **Observer names are public**, read live from `profiles.display_name`. Never
    from `alerts.observer_name`: the alert log keeps that name after an account
    is deleted, so publishing it would keep publishing the name of someone who
    asked to be erased. A missing profile renders "En medlem".
    (`src/lib/alert-observer.ts`.)
  - **All 238 larm are published**, and the administrator can hide any single
    one at `/admin/larm` — an opt-out, a protected species or a mistake. It
    disappears from the feed, the home page and the photo route.
  - **Place: lokal name → nearest lokal within 3 km ("Nära Köphultasjö") →
    "Markaryds kommun" → "Utanför kommunen".** An exact coordinate is never
    published. Measured on the live data, that puts 190 of 238 larm on the
    "nära" rung and only 7 on the vague kommun rung.
  - **The protected-species list is still empty**, and the hook that uses it is
    wired up (`PLACE_SUPPRESSED_SPECIES` in `src/lib/alert-place.ts`): a species
    in it never gets a place finer than the kommun, lokal name included. The
    list itself needs the club's input, following Artportalen's skyddsklassade
    arter, **before launch**.

### D2 — Admin sign-in

**Decided 2026-09-19, shipped in step 2:** exactly **one administrator** signs
in on the web, with **email and password**. There's no member login.

- The administrator is Jonas's existing app account
  (`yunus@edenmind.com`) with `profiles.is_admin = true`, granted by
  SQL. That flag is shared with the app, where it also allows creating lokaler
  (the `locations` insert policy). No second admin concept.
- Why password and not Google: the Google provider has no OAuth secret (it only
  serves the app's native ID tokens), so web Google sign-in would need a change
  to the shared auth config and the Google Cloud console. Password sign-in uses
  the email provider as it is, like `../mhf`: no email delivery, no callback URL,
  no auth config change.
- The password is set with `pnpm admin:password <email>`, run by the user in
  their own terminal, so it never passes through a chat or a log.
- The gate reads `profiles.is_admin` with the user's own session. The column is
  readable by signed-in users; only writing it is revoked. RLS on the site's
  tables (`private.is_admin()`) stays the real boundary.

### D3 — Where site media lives

This covers article images, page images and PDFs. It does **not** cover the
app's photos, which stay in `user-uploads`.

**Decided 2026-09-19:** a new **public Supabase Storage bucket**,
`site-media`, owned by this repo, with **pre-generated widths**.

- Public read; writes need `private.is_admin()`. Auth and RLS are already in
  place, and it needs no new Cloudflare binding.
- **R2 isn't needed.** The organisation is on **Supabase Pro** (100 GB of
  storage included), and the whole WP library is about 0.45 GB.
- **Three widths, generated once** (e.g. 480, 960 and 1600 px, WebP), served
  with `srcset`: browser-side in the admin at upload, `sharp` in the import
  script. Pro also offers on-the-fly transforms (100 source images a month,
  then $5 per 1,000), but pre-generated widths cost nothing per image and
  don't depend on that service.
- Open until the import (step 8): keep the PDFs (Cinclus archive) public? They
  are public on WordPress today.

### D4 — How published content renders

- **Rebuild-on-publish is ruled out by local-only shipping.** Prerendering
  articles would need a build and deploy on every publish, which means CI or
  Workers Builds deploying.
**Decided 2026-09-19: all public HTML goes through the Worker.**

- **Server-render DB content** (articles, pages, events, the home page, the
  menu) per request in the Worker, with a short `Cache-Control` so publishing
  shows up within a minute.
  - Sanitize HTML on **save**, not on render, to stay inside the Workers Free
    CPU budget (10 ms per request).
  - Pages that read nothing from the database (the 404, the legal pages) stay
    prerendered.
- **Routing:** `run_worker_first` becomes `["/*", "!/_astro/*", …]`, with an
  exclusion for each static file or folder. It accepts `!` patterns, which take
  precedence, and at most 100 entries. The alternative, fixed SSR prefixes
  (`/artiklar/*`, `/sidor/*`) with everything else static, would have forced
  pages under a prefix and the menu into code.
- **Consequence for redirects:** Cloudflare applies `_redirects` only to
  requests served as assets, **never to requests the Worker serves**. The old
  URLs are therefore redirected by the Worker (see D6).
- **Caching.** Step 3 sends `Cache-Control: public, max-age=60` and caches
  nothing at the edge. Astro 7's route cache with the Cloudflare provider
  (`cacheCloudflare()`, Workers Cache) could serve cached pages without
  running the Worker and purge by tag on publish. It works on `workers.dev`,
  but once enabled it bills **every** request against the Workers quota,
  static assets included (checked 2026-09-19). Decide at launch, with real
  traffic numbers.
- **Watch:** Workers Free allows 100 000 requests a day, plenty for a club
  site. The $5 plan removes the CPU and request caps if needed.

### D5 — Editing and the HTML policy

"Generell html-redigering" means a WYSIWYG editor like WordPress's, plus an HTML
source view.

- **Recommendation: Tiptap** (headless ProseMirror, fits React and shadcn),
  storing **HTML**.
  - Sanitize server-side on save with an allowlist, since a stolen admin
    session must not be able to plant scripts.
  - Render with `set:html`.
  - Imported WP HTML goes through the same sanitizer.
- **Embeds** come in through an allowlist of iframe hosts, and each host is
  added to the CSP `frame-src`. Today's CSP has none, so it falls back to
  `default-src 'self'` and blocks every cross-origin iframe.
  - The content only uses **Artportalen** and **Google Maps** (the lokal
    pages), so those two start the allowlist. Other hosts are added when a
    real need turns up.
  - The two one-off embeds in old posts (natursidan.se, smof.se) become plain
    links at import.
- **The sanitizer is `sanitize-html`** (measured 2026-09-19): about 0.8 ms
  for a 6 KB article once warm, and 24 ms cold for a deliberately huge 89 KB
  page in workerd. Workers Free allows 10 ms of CPU, so saving an enormous
  page could be refused; `xss` is about 2.5× faster if that ever happens.
- **Decided 2026-09-19: drafts + scheduled publishing, no revision history.**
  Every article and page has a status (draft or published) and a publish time,
  which may be in the future; the item stays hidden until then. With server
  rendering, that's a `where` clause.

### D6 — URLs and redirects

Old URLs must keep working: 353 posts at `/<slug>/`, pages at nested paths,
inbound links, search results, and the app's legal links.

**Decided 2026-09-19** (together with D4):

- **Pages keep their WordPress paths** (e.g. `/om-foreningen/medlemskap`), so
  they need no redirects.
- **Articles move to `/artiklar/<slug>`, keeping the WordPress slug.**
  Swedish prefixes throughout (`/artiklar`, `/kalender`, `/lokaler`).
- **Old post URLs get a 301 from the Worker**, looked up in a generated map
  that a unit test covers. It can't be `_redirects`, which the Worker bypasses
  (see D4). The map is written in step 8 by the import.
- The two legal pages stay **static `.astro` pages at their exact old URLs**,
  both forms. They change rarely, belong in version control, and then never
  depend on the database.
- Trailing slashes: the plan chooses one canonical form and redirects the
  other, since every WordPress URL ends in `/`.

### D7 — What "i kommunen" means

Larmligan counts species seen **in Markaryds kommun**, but observations carry
free coordinates from anywhere.

- **Built in step 4, and already in the repo:** `isInsideKommun` in
  `src/lib/alert-place.ts`, a ray-casting check against
  `src/data/markaryd-boundary.json`.
  - That file is **OSM relation 935621** (`admin_level=7`, `ref:scb=0767`),
    fetched from Overpass, its main ring simplified to about 30 m, **plus the
    kommun's three sub-kilometre exclaves**, which a coarser simplification
    erased entirely. 8 KB, ODbL, © OpenStreetMap contributors. The SOS export
    (`/Areas/Municipality/767/Export`) was the first choice and needs an API key
    we don't have.
  - Verified against Markaryd, Strömsnäsbruk, Traryd and Timsfors (inside) and
    Älmhult, Ljungby, Hässleholm, Osby, Malmö and Dublin (outside).
  - Hundreds of points a year need no PostGIS. Enabling an extension on the
    shared database is avoidable.
  - Observations at a named lokal count if the lokal is inside.
- ~~**Open:** count **observations** (every report) or only **alerts**?~~
  **Moot since 2026-09-20**: Larmligan comes from Artportalen (F6), so no
  league is computed from the club's own data and nothing has to decide between
  the two. The boundary check itself stays in use by the larm place ladder
  (`coarsePlace`), which is what D7 was really needed for.

## Features

Each lists the agenda item, the proposed shape, dependencies and open questions.

### F1 — Statiska sidor (pages)

- Admin-edited pages in a page tree (parent + order) that also drives the
  navigation menu. They cover about 20 of today's pages, migrated with F2's
  import. The Artportalen stubs become menu links.
- **New table:** `pages` (slug, parent, title, body HTML, status, order,
  timestamps).
- **Depends on:** D2, D4, D5, D6.
- **Decided 2026-09-19: the menu is built from the page tree.**
  - Each page has a parent, an order and a "visa i menyn" flag.
  - Link-only entries (Artportalen's lists) sit in the same tree.
  - Pages are reordered in `/admin`. There is no separate menu editor.
  - Step 3's design pass proposes a structure based on today's menu, and the
    user signs it off.

### F2 — Artiklar

- Articles (title, slug, excerpt, body, cover image, category, author name,
  published date, status), a public list with categories and paging, and the
  admin CRUD.
- **Dummy articles for now.** The WordPress import comes later (decided
  2026-09-19). Until then, build and test against a handful of dummy articles:
  - varied on purpose: with and without images, one long, several categories;
  - clearly marked (e.g. a slug prefix `exempel-`);
  - **deleted before the import**.
- **The import (later)** is a one-off, idempotent script (`scripts/wp-import/`,
  keyed on the WP id) that:
  - reads the public REST API **one request at a time, with a pause between
    requests**, because the host blocks bursts. Drafts and private posts need
    an application password.
  - downloads own-host images into `site-media` in three widths;
  - rewrites `<img>` URLs;
  - sanitizes the HTML;
  - writes the old-URL redirect map that the Worker serves (D6).

  A human runs it locally with a secret key in an uncommitted env file, first
  as a dry run.
- **Category cleanup:** 45 categories to a handful, mapped in a table the club
  signs off.
- **New tables:** `articles`, `categories` (or a text enum), maybe `media`.
- **Depends on:** D2–D6, and F1's editor.
- **Answered 2026-09-20:**
  - **Hotlinked external images: copy the ones that still answer** into
    `site-media` and rewrite the URL; **drop the `<img>` for any that are
    dead.** The article then survives its source site disappearing, and the
    page stops telling `vinterfaglar.se` and the others that someone read it.
    The importer logs what it copied and what it dropped, per article.
  - **Unattached media: import exactly what the content references.**
    WordPress records only the post a file was *uploaded* to, so "unattached"
    does not mean unused — a body can reference one by URL. The importer walks
    every body anyway, so it takes what is referenced and skips the rest,
    which avoids both broken images and dead weight.
  - **The 57 PDFs (Cinclus) stay public**, as they are on WordPress today. No
    new exposure, and it closes D3's last open item. (Keeping them behind
    `/admin` was rejected: there is no member login (D2), so members who use
    the archive would simply lose it.)
  - **Original publish dates and authors are kept.**
- **Open, before the import:** the **category mapping** — 45 WordPress
  categories down to a handful. It needs the club's sign-off, so it has lead
  time and should be raised with them now.

### F3 — Kalender (events)

- Events (title, start/end, all-day, place as free text or a `locations` lokal,
  description HTML, category, maybe a registration link). They replace both the
  "Program" posts and the hand-edited Årsprogram page.
- Public: upcoming list plus month view, and each event has a page.
- **Suggested extra: an iCal feed** (`/kalender.ics`) that anyone can subscribe
  to in their phone calendar. It's almost free to build.
- Admin CRUD.
- **New table:** `events`.
- **Depends on:** D2, D4, D5.

**Decided 2026-09-20** (the three open questions, plus what planning settled):

- **No recurring events.** One row per occurrence; the admin copies an event for
  the next time. No RRULE to expand in three views, and no policy for changing a
  single occurrence.
- **Old "Program" posts stay articles.** The import (step 8) would have to read
  dates out of free text in 16 years of posts. The calendar holds what the admin
  puts in it from here on, and replaces the hand-edited Årsprogram page.
- **The iCal feed is built now** (`/kalender.ics`), so members can subscribe in
  their own phone calendar.
- **The app may read events later.** `events` gets a `to anon` select policy like
  `articles`, so no schema change is needed when it does. No app work now.
- **URL `/kalender`, menu item "Program"** — the slug is already reserved in
  `src/lib/slug.ts`, and "Program" is the word the club uses (see the menu
  proposal in `docs/design.md`).
- **An event has a type from a fixed list**, stored as a checked column:
  exkursion, möte, resa, kurs, tävling, arbetsdag, övrigt. Not the article
  `categories` table, which holds Referat and Föreningsaktuellt.
- **The public view is a date list grouped by month**, not a grid: it follows
  the fältdagbok direction, reads the same at 360 px, and needs no JS.
- **Place is free text**, not a foreign key into the app's `locations`. F5 can
  add a soft link later.
- **No cover image.** Adding one would mean re-policying `media`, whose anon
  policy only exposes article covers. Inline images in the description still
  work.

**How it ended up:**

- `/kalender` (upcoming, grouped by month), `/kalender/2026-10` (one month,
  with the neighbouring months linked), `/kalender/<slug>`, `/kalender.ics`,
  and the next event on the home page above the lead.
- The `events` table copies the articles publishing model exactly, so
  `src/lib/publish-state.ts` drives drafts and scheduling unchanged, and a
  draft is invisible because of the `to anon` policy rather than a filter in a
  query.
- **A month URL and an event slug share one route.** `2026-10` is a valid slug
  shape, so `/kalender/[slug].astro` checks the month pattern first and
  `parseEventForm` refuses to give an event a name of that shape. Both halves
  are tested.
- `src/lib/ical.ts` is pure and tested because iCalendar is unforgiving in ways
  a browser never shows: CRLF endings, folding at 75 **octets** (not
  characters, so å counts twice), and `;` `,` `\` escaped inside text. An
  all-day event's `DTEND` is the day after it finishes, because that field is
  exclusive.
- An event with no end time gets **no `DTEND`**. RFC 5545 reads that as ending
  when it starts; inventing a length would put a wrong time in someone's
  calendar.
- The feed is cached for an hour: calendar clients poll on their own schedule
  and ignore anything shorter.

### F4 — Larm (public alert feed, with photos) — *viktig funktion*

**Decided 2026-09-19:** display only. The site never sends alerts.

- The app's alert feed on the public site: species, count, time, place,
  rarity, **photo**, and the observer if D1 allows. It has a detail view with a
  small map.
- Reads `alerts` and joins `observations.image_path` through `observation_id`
  (21 alerts have no observation left, so no photo).
- Everything goes through D1's public-data module, with the secret key,
  curated fields, and photos through a Worker route. It's all existing data,
  with **no migration**.
- Keyset paging, since Supabase caps responses at 1 000 rows.

**Shipped 2026-09-20.** How it ended up:

- `/larm` (12 per page, keyset on `(sent_at, id)`), `/larm/<uuid>`,
  `/larm/bild/<uuid>` for the photo, and the four newest on the home page.
- The public routes read through **`src/lib/public-alerts.ts`, the only module
  that imports the secret key** (`src/lib/supabase-secret.ts`). Pages get
  `PublicAlert` DTOs and never hold the RLS-bypassing client.
  `secret-key-scope.test.ts` fails the build if that stops being true, if an
  island reaches it, or if a larm route forgets `prerender = false` — which
  matters because `astro build` prerenders inside workerd with `.dev.vars`
  loaded, so a prerendered larm page would bake member data into `dist/`.
- `alert-dto.test.ts` pins the published field set and the `select` string, and
  asserts that sentinel values for user id, `image_path` and the coordinates
  don't survive `JSON.stringify`.
- The photo route **streams** the object out of the private `user-uploads`
  bucket (not `storage.download()`, which buffers up to 3 MB) and answers one
  identical 404 for a bad id, an unknown larm, a hidden larm, a report without a
  picture and a missing object, so it is not an oracle for what has been hidden.
- Rarity is shown as a **word, only from "Ovanlig" up**, coloured only for the
  two rarest levels. Nothing in the first 238 larm was level 0 or 1, and 191
  were level 4–5, so a badge on every row would have meant nothing.
- **Open:** the protected-species list, before launch (see D1 and step 9).

### F5 — Lokaler (map)

- A public map of lokaler with their larm history, like the app's Lokaler
  screen. It shows the 61 named lokaler and the alert pins at D1's precision,
  and each lokal lists its alerts.
- A React island (`client:visible`) with Leaflet or MapLibre. Tiles from
  OpenStreetMap or Lantmäteriet's open topographic map. Tile hosts go into the
  CSP.
- **Depends on:** D1 and F4's public-data module.
- **Answered 2026-09-20** (measured on the live data the same day):
  - **All 61 lokaler get a pin**, and one with a larm in the last 14 days is
    marked as active — the app's own Lokaler code, so the two agree. The map is
    "var kan man skåda i kommunen", not only "var har det larmats". **No
    clustering**: 61 pins fit the kommun at one zoom, and clustering would hide
    precisely the lokaler in tätorten. Lokaler sharing a point (Ängabäck and
    Ängabäcks kraftstation) are drawn as one pin that names both.
  - **Never a larm pin.** D1 forbids publishing the coordinate, so a larm is
    only ever drawn at the lokal it resolves to.
  - **A lokal page lists its larm under two headings.** "På lokalen" uses the
    app's rule — within `LOKAL_RADIUS_M` (300 m) of the lokal, or the reporter
    picked it — which is 78 larm across 21 lokaler. "I närheten" holds the rest
    of the 3 km `NEAR_KM` band, 133 more larm across 36 lokaler, the ones
    `/larm` already labels "Nära X". Neither the app nor the site's own place
    labels get contradicted, and no larm is dropped. (27 larm resolve to no
    lokal at all and appear on none.)
  - **Every lokal gets a page**, `/lokaler/<slug>`, with its larm, its position
    and its description. **The 9 WP lokal pages 301 there** in step 8's redirect
    map, and their text becomes the lokal's description — only 4 of the 61 have
    one today. `locations.description` belongs to the app, so the site
    **never writes it**; the text is carried over by hand, with the user's
    approval, as a shared-database change.
  - **Tiles: standard OpenStreetMap raster, through Leaflet.** No key, no
    registration, one host in the CSP. Lantmäteriet's topographic map is the
    better birding base and stays the upgrade if the club registers a token.

**Built 2026-09-20.** Two things the answers above got wrong, both found by
looking at the real data and the rendered page:

- **"På lokalen" is nearest-wins, and only one lokal ever wins.** The first
  build counted a larm as *at* every lokal within 300 m, so one evening at the
  Tröskeln was filed at three neighbouring lokaler at once, and Rallvägen's page
  listed larm whose own place label read "Nära Reningsdammarna". The app had
  already settled this, in its own words: *"a pin between two lokaler belongs to
  the closer one, never to both."* The site now uses the same rule
  (`homeLokalId` in `src/lib/lokal-alerts.ts`), so the 78 "at" larm partition
  across 22 lokaler instead of being counted several times over.
- **The register's tally counts only those larm**, not the three-kilometre band.
  109 larm lie within 3 km of Broarna i Timsfors and 9 came from it; publishing
  109 would rank every lokal in tätorten identically off the same reports.

Also settled while building: the map is near-square rather than wide (the
kommun is 19 km by 18, so a wide frame only zooms out past Laholm and Osby);
"På lokalen" prints no place, because the heading has said it; "I närheten"
stops at twelve; and the island imports Leaflet **inside its effect**, since
Leaflet reads `window` in its module body and an `.astro` page importing the
component otherwise pulls it into the Worker bundle and fails the route.

The design is in `docs/design.md`, "Lokaler".

### F6 — Larmligan

**Decided 2026-09-20: the league comes from Artportalen, not from the club's
own larm.** It is Artportalen's *Observatörsligan* for Markaryds kommun — the
same list the WordPress site links to today — and not a leaderboard computed
from `alerts` or `observations`.

What that changes:

- **It stops being a shared-database feature.** No member names, no
  observations, no `profiles` join, no secret key, nothing from F4's
  public-data module. The one part of the agenda that would have ranked members
  by their own reports now ranks Artportalen accounts on Artportalen's numbers,
  which is the club's existing practice and nobody's private data.
- **It no longer depends on D1 or D7.** D7's open sub-question — count
  observations or alerts — is moot; the kommun boundary it also covers is still
  in use by the larm place ladder.
- **It moves under F7**, which is where the club's other Artportalen lists
  live. The "keep Observatörsligan as a link or drop it" question below is
  answered by this: Observatörsligan *is* Larmligan.

- **Open: a link, or a table rendered on the site?** Checked 2026-09-20 against
  `biodiversitydata-se/SOS`:
  - **The names are there.** `occurrence.recordedBy` ("names of people, groups
    or organizations responsible for recording the original Occurrence") and
    `occurrence.reportedBy` ("name of the person that reported the species
    observation") are documented fields of the observation model. So a table is
    not blocked for want of data.
  - **But there is no aggregation by observer.** The only aggregation endpoints
    are `Observations_GeogridAggregation` and `Observations_TaxonAggregation`
    (`Docs/Endpoints.md`). Årslista and Markarydslistan are one
    `TaxonAggregation` call each; a league is not.
  - **So a table means a full scan of a kommun-year**, paged through the search
    endpoint and counted in the Worker — against a **hard limit of 10 000
    records, 1 000 per page** (`Docs/FAQ.md`). Whether Markaryds kommun's bird
    year fits under that is **one measurement away and needs the API key**: the
    search response's total count answers it in a single call.
  - **And it would be our ranking, not Artportalen's.** Counting raw
    observations ourselves can disagree with the official Observatörsligan over
    validation status, taxon scope and protected observations. Two pages
    claiming to be the same list and disagreeing is worse than one link — which
    is exactly the test F7's spike already applies to the species lists.
  - **Recommendation: link at launch**, and add this question to that spike.
    One key, one afternoon, three lists answered. Going native later costs
    nothing that the link forecloses.
  - **Worth the club's view either way:** a ranked list of named individuals is
    personal data. Artportalen publishes it; republishing a ranking we computed
    is a different act from linking to theirs.

### F7 — Årslista and Kommunlista (Artportalen)

These are today's redirects: this year's species list for the kommun, and
*Markarydslistan* (every species ever recorded in the kommun, with the first
date).

- **Phase A, at launch (parity):** links or redirects to the same Artportalen
  pages, with the year computed. That removes the New Year chore.
- **Phase B, native:**
  - an SSR route that makes **one** `TaxonAggregation` call per list (kommun

    sighting;
  - caches the answer (Cache API, refreshed in the background every few hours)
    and renders it in the site's design, with "Källa: Artportalen";

  - **They match, or only differ by protected species:** go native. A missing
    protected species can be noted ("exkl. skyddsklassade fynd") or added by

  - **They differ a lot:** keep linking to Artportalen, the authoritative list.
- **The spike also answers Larmligan (F6).** Two extra questions, same key,

  1. **How many bird observations does the kommun have in a year?** One search

     in the Worker; over it, and the hard cap settles the matter — a link.
  2. **If it fits, does our count match Artportalen's Observatörsligan?** Same
     test as the species lists: match, or differ only by protected observations,
     and a table is honest; differ a lot and we would be publishing a second,
     contradictory ranking of named people under the club's name.
- **Observatörsligan** (Artportalen's user top list) **is Larmligan**, decided
  2026-09-20 (F6). It is kept, as a link, and it carries the agenda's
  "larmligan — vem som har sett flest arter i kommunen i år". Note it is
  Artportalen's ranking of Artportalen accounts, so it answers that question
  for the kommun rather than for the club's membership.
